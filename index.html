    About

    I am an undergraduate student at POSTECH, double major in computer science and mathematics. My primary research interests are programming languages, logic, software verification, software engineering, and distributed systems.

    I am currently working at POSTECH Software Verification Laboratory as an undergraduate research intern, advised by Professor Kyungmin Bae. My current research topics are rewriting logic-based model checking techniques. See Publications for more information.

    I am also a member of PDAO, a blockchain-based decentralized autonomous organization in POSTECH. I designed the consensus protocol of its blockchain engine, Simperby. For a bit more information, you can see my seminar on Youtube (in Korean). You can find my seminar on YouTube (in Korean)

    During 2020 and 2021, I worked as a software engineer at Kodebox. I developed CodeChain Foundry, a blockchain engine, and Zuzu, a web-based business management software.

    Before then, I worked at POSTECH Programming Language Laboratory as an undergraduate research intern, advised by Professor Sungwoo Park. I participated in developing MR3, a general-purposed distributed execution framework.
