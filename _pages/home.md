# About 

I am an undergraduate student at [POSTECH](https://www.postech.ac.kr/eng/), double major in computer science and mathematics. 
My primary research interests are programming languages, logic, software verification, software engineering, and distributed systems.

I am currently working at [POSTECH Software Verification Laboratory](http://sv.postech.ac.kr/) as an undergraduate research intern, 
advised by Professor [Kyungmin Bae](http://sv.postech.ac.kr/~kmbae/).
My current research topics are rewriting logic-based model checking techniques.
See [Publications](/publications) for more information.

I am also a member of [PDAO](https://github.com/postech-dao), a blockchain-based decentralized autonomous organization in POSTECH.
I designed the consensus protocol of its blockchain engine, [Simperby](https://github.com/postech-dao/simperby).
For a bit more information, you can see [my seminar on Youtube (in Korean)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpMdgifwE6Y).
You can find my seminar on YouTube (in Korean)

During 2020 and 2021, I worked as a software engineer at [Kodebox](https://kodebox.io/).
I developed [CodeChain Foundry](https://github.com/CodeChain-io/foundry), a blockchain engine,
and [Zuzu](https://zuzu.network/), a web-based business management software.

Before then, I worked at [POSTECH Programming Language Laboratory](http://pl.postech.ac.kr/) as an undergraduate research intern,
advised by Professor [Sungwoo Park](http://pl.postech.ac.kr/~gla/).
I participated in developing [MR3](https://www.datamonad.com/), a general-purposed distributed execution framework.
